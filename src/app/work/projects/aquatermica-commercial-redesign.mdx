## Overview

AQUATERMICA is a family business in Lima, Peru, specializing in water-heating systems and pumps. The sales area needed a structured annual plan — instead of guessing, I used AI to analyze historical data and build a data-driven sales strategy.

## The Analysis

Processed **1,476 historical quotes** spanning several years to extract patterns in:

- Customer segments and their conversion rates
- Product mix and margin distribution
- Seasonal demand patterns
- Quote-to-sale conversion bottlenecks
- Pricing competitiveness by product category

## AI-Assisted Strategy

Using Claude AI as an analytical partner, combined with Python and pandas for data processing, the analysis produced:

- **19 prioritized action items** ranked by impact and implementation difficulty
- **17 of the 19 actions required zero additional investment** — they were process improvements, pricing adjustments, and communication changes that cost nothing to implement
- An **8-chapter master document** covering the complete commercial strategy: from customer acquisition to after-sales service

## Deliverables

1. Complete data analysis report with visualizations
2. Strategic action plan with priority matrix
3. 8-chapter master document for the commercial area
4. Implementation timeline with quick wins identified

## Technologies Used

- **Claude AI** — Strategic analysis and document generation
- **Python + pandas** — Data processing and statistical analysis
- **Excel** — Data cleaning, visualization, and final report formatting

## Impact

Transformed a "gut feel" commercial operation into a data-informed strategy. The zero-cost emphasis meant the business could start improving immediately without capital investment — a critical factor for a family-run operation.

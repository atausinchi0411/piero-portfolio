## Overview

Built an automated data analytics pipeline that processes weekly equipment performance data from SAP, cross-references it with operational records, and produces calibrated scoring reports that help maintenance engineers prioritize interventions and detect equipment misuse patterns.

## How It Works

1. **Data extraction**: Weekly automated pull from SAP containing maintenance records, work orders, and equipment telemetry.
2. **Cross-referencing**: The pipeline matches equipment events against operational context — shift logs, environmental conditions, and historical baselines — to distinguish genuine failures from operator misuse.
3. **Calibrated scoring**: A custom scoring model weights each event type by severity, frequency, and cost impact, producing a normalized score per piece of equipment.
4. **Report generation**: Automated Excel reports with cost-per-ton breakdowns, trend visualizations, and anomaly flags for the entire fleet.

## Technical Details

- **Python + pandas**: Core data processing and transformation logic
- **SAP integration**: Structured data extraction from SAP maintenance modules
- **Excel/VBA**: Output reports formatted for management consumption, with embedded charts and conditional formatting
- **Scoring algorithm**: Custom weighted model that accounts for equipment type, operational context, and historical failure rates

## Fleet Coverage

The pipeline monitors **13+ pieces of heavy mining equipment**, each with different maintenance profiles, failure modes, and cost structures. The scoring model is calibrated per equipment type to ensure fair comparison.

## Impact

- Early detection of misuse patterns that were previously invisible in raw SAP data
- Quantified cost-per-ton metrics that enabled evidence-based maintenance budget allocation
- Weekly automated reports replaced a manual process that previously took a full day of engineering time
